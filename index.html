HOW TO INTERPRET IT

A couple of things worth knowing.

01 / Match your filters

Use the same site, dates, countries and devices in both reports. Compare equivalent periods where possible.

02 / Don't add the figures together

AI feature impressions are included in total Google Web impressions. This is a presence ratio, not two separate audiences.

03 / Look at absolute numbers too

A rising rate can occur even as AI impressions fall, if total search impressions fall faster.
